# Writing Style Guidelines for Academic Chapters

- **Natural Burstiness & Perplexity:** Avoid uniform sentence structures, robotic transitions, and repetitive phrasing. Vary sentence lengths organically—mix short, punchy technical statements with more detailed explanatory sentences.
- **Human Academic Tone:** Write like an experienced German engineering researcher writing an IEEE/VDE paper. Use precise technical vocabulary instead of overly generic AI filler words (e.g., "zudem", "darüber hinaus", "es ist wichtig zu betonen").
- **Direct & Rigorous:** Focus strictly on methodological precision, mathematical rigor (DDQN, AWGN models), and empirical results. Avoid generic introductory fluff.
- **Avoid Overused AI Transitional Vocabulary:** Strictly avoid textbook AI transition words and filler phrases that trigger detectors, such as: "zudem", "darüber hinaus", "es ist wichtig zu betonen", "festzuhalten ist", "im Hinblick auf", "insgesamt lässt sich sagen", "als KI", and excessive use of passive voice where active engineering phrasing works better.
- **Syntactic Variation & Structural Asymmetry:** Do not write paragraphs with uniform lengths. Intentionally break the rhythm by alternating between complex subordinate clauses containing mathematical dependencies and abrupt, highly specific declarative statements.
- **Terminology Anchoring:** Ground every paragraph in concrete domain-specific entities (e.g., specific state spaces, reward functions, action spaces, SNR decibels, packet loss ratios) rather than abstract, floating conceptual descriptions.
